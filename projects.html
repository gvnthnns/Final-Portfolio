Project 2

RISC V Multicycle Processor

Description: Designed and implemented a multicycle processor by explicitly connecting its datapath components and control signals. The design represents how instructions move through processor hardware over multiple cycles.

My role: Implemented processor logic at the signal and wire level, connecting components such as registers, the ALU, and control logic so they work together during instruction execution.

Skills or knowledge gained: VHDL, digital logic, processor architecture, multicycle control, datapath design, and debugging hardware descriptions.

Resources used: VHDL, course materials, processor diagrams, and debugging support.
