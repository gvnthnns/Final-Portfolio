Senior Design Project

AI Arcade Cabinet

Description: The AI Arcade is designed to help people who cannot code modify their favorite retro games or create entirely new ones using AI. A player describes the game or changes they want through a terminal interface. The AI then generates the code, launches the resulting game, and lets the player try it on the arcade cabinet.

My role: I am on the software team, implementing the AI systems on the Raspberry Pi. My work helps connect player requests from the terminal to the AI functionality that creates or modifies games.

Skills or knowledge gained: I am developing experience integrating AI systems with Raspberry Pi hardware, connecting software components, testing system behavior, and collaborating on a multidisciplinary team.

Big picture contribution: The project makes game creation and modification more accessible to people without coding experience. Integrating the AI system into the arcade cabinet helps turn a natural language request into a game that users can immediately play and explore.
